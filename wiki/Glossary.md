# Glossary

[`DEFINITIONS.md`](https://github.com/mearvk/Ubuntu.Determinant.Beta.Restricted/blob/main/DEFINITIONS.md)
(Ubuntu White Edition; attention: Max Rupplin — MEARVK LLC — 2026). It defines
both the project's coined vocabulary and the terms used by the vendored-source
documentation set. This page is a quick index; `DEFINITIONS.md` is definitive.

> *scape*, and *net universe* are deliberate software-modeling metaphors — not
> measurements of human or machine intelligence, worth, or physical reality.
