# Proffer & Core Vocabulary

**Proffer** is the project's central framing: state should carry not just its
value but its *story* — origin, transition, capability, permission, and what to
examine next.

> A modern secure runtime should know not only what it is doing, but **where the
> state came from, what transition produced it, what capability it exercises,
> why the transition is permitted, and what should be examined next.**

Proffer source lives under
[`proffer/`](https://github.com/mearvk/Ubuntu.Determinant.Beta.Restricted/tree/main/proffer).

## Core vocabulary

These are software-modeling constructs (see the reader's-note caution on the
[Home](Home) page — the metaphors are not human/physical measurements).

| Term | Meaning (as used in the project) |
|---|---|
| **Proffer** | Evidence-and-provenance-carrying account of a runtime state and the transition that produced it. |
| **Fielter** | A filtering/field construct in the core vocabulary (see `README.md` § Fielter). |
| **Exact Fall** | A precise, fully-determined transition/derivation of state. |
| **Call Fall** | A transition mediated through a call boundary. |
| **300-IQ framing** | A design metaphor for a high-context, self-explaining system — **not** an intelligence measurement. |

## The 300-IQ framing

The "300 IQ" label names a design aspiration: a system that models its own
context richly enough to explain its state and choices. Per the project's own
definitions, it is a metaphor for software modeling depth, **not** a claim about
human or machine intelligence.
