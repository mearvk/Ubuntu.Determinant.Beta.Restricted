# Legal & Provenance

The project treats provenance and attribution with the same discipline it
applies to runtime evidence: origin, licensing, custody, and uncertainty are
recorded explicitly.

## Attribution & upstream discipline

| Document | Role |
|---|---|
| `HEADINGS.md` | New code authored for this repository is the work of *Max Rupplin — MEARVK LLC — 2026*; defines the per-file heading forms. |
| `MODIFICATIONS.md` | Repository-wide record of how each vendored source tree differs from upstream (Additions, deletions, changes). |
| `tools/git/FOUNDING.md` | Founds the relationship to a vendored third-party source tree: what is vendored, provenance, what is/isn't modified, license, re-acquisition. |
| `SIGNING.md`, `SHA256.md` | Signing procedure and integrity sums. |

## Legal materials (`legal/`)

- `LICENSE.legal` — license text.
- `US_ATTORNEY_HOLD.md` — an **internal** attribution / identity / clean-record
  and evidence-preservation framework.

> **Important (from the document itself):** the U.S. Attorney Hold is internal
> preservation/evidence recordkeeping. It is **not** a court order, governmental
> hold, university determination, attorney-client communication, or proof of any
> person's license or employment. External legal status must be established from
> the appropriate primary authority.

## International Criminal Court materials (`international-criminal-court/`)

Contains `ABOUT-THE-COURT.md`, `INTEGRITY.md`, `SIGNING.md`, `SHA256SUMS`,
`SignatorySeries.md`, and certification/standing documents. These are project
records and signing artifacts.

> **Caution.** These are repository documents and integrity artifacts. Any
> real-world legal standing, certification, or institutional relationship must be
> verified against the relevant primary authority — the presence of a document
> here does not establish external legal fact. This mirrors the project's own
> rule: *the existence of an input is not proof of truth.*

## Data provenance

The world statistical archive (`countries/MASTER.md`, `national/<country>/`)
records, per country, its data sources and pull status, with per-country
`DATA_SOURCES.md`. Derived data is kept distinguishable from primary sources.
